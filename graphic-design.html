Game development

Godot · GDScript · Private personal project

A small preview of a private personal project I built to learn Godot and GDScript. Every asset was created by me, including the character, her expressions, the scenery and the props, and I learned how to make animated sprites along the way. The result is a fully working mini game: the character walks through different levels, interacts with objects and finishes with a short pop quiz. I also learned how to publish a game on itch.io.
