How I work

Curious by nature

I’m naturally curious and enjoy understanding how things work beneath the surface. When I encounter something unfamiliar, I like exploring it rather than being intimidated by not knowing it yet.

Learn by building

I learn best through practical projects. Writing code, designing interfaces, debugging problems and experimenting with new technologies help me turn concepts into skills.

Creative problem solver

I enjoy approaching problems from both a technical and creative perspective. I’m interested not only in whether something works, but also in whether the experience is clear, thoughtful and enjoyable.

Always improving

I don’t expect to know everything. Software development is constantly evolving, and I enjoy being in a field where there is always something new to learn, improve and explore.

My learning journey

I began my development journey by building a foundation in programming and software development, gradually expanding into web, mobile and full-stack development.

I’m still at the beginning of my professional journey, but I’m actively building the skills and portfolio I want to take into the industry.
