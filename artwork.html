My art journey

My art journey began in 2019, at the age of fourteen. Curious about the world and still learning how to navigate it, I started by drawing familiar references and adding my own twists, to find out whether I had the skill or whether, like many people, art simply wasn’t for me.

Every small line and drop of ink grew my passion and curiosity. I began in a well-worn notebook, trying different styles and techniques while searching for a place where I belonged. Over time I was drawn to work that felt intentional and meaningful, exploring plain black ink, watercolour, acrylic paint, alcohol markers and oil pastels.

Every mistake, every weak attempt and every piece I’m proud of has shaped the artist I am today. That creativity later merged with my passion for software, where I found a place to be both technical and creative: graphic design.

From there I began working as a graphic designer, completing a range of projects for companies. Working digitally opened up a new world of art, and it blends naturally with my software development skills, allowing my full potential to show.

I’m proud and grateful for how far I’ve come since 2019. This portfolio, my GitHub README and my CV were all designed by me.
